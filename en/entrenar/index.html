How it works

1 · Estudiar

You see the solver's full chart, with mixed hands in two colours.

2 · Calcar

The chart is shown faded and you paint over it, cell by cell or by dragging.

3 · From memory

No help. With 97% accuracy the range “warms up” and its next review moves further away.

4 · Hand by hand

Series of 10 random hands from the range, as at the table, with the solver's answer.

Your progress is saved in this browser and, if you sign in with Google in My account, on all your devices.

Why spaced repetition works

Reviewing a range just before you forget it fixes it far better than rereading it ten times in a row. That is why the trainer schedules each review: score 97% or more and the next review moves further away; miss and it comes back sooner. Redrawing the chart from memory (active recall) is what turns a range into a reflex at the table.

Frequently asked questions

How much time does it take?

About 5 minutes a day: the “To review today” list tells you which ranges are due.

Can I train my own ranges?

Yes: create a range by painting the chart or pasting text such as “22+, A2s+, KTo+” and train it like the solver's.

Is my progress saved?

In your browser, and in the cloud if you sign in with Google from My account.
