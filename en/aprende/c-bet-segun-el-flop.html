The c-bet by flop: size and frequency

The continuation bet (c-bet) is the most repeated play in poker. At real tournament tables, a third-pot c-bet makes more than a third of opponents fold, and a bluff of that size only needs 25% folds.

Texture rules

  • Flops altos and secos (A72, K83): favour the preflop raiser. Small c-bet with almost everything.
  • Flops medios conectados (987, T86): connect with the blind's defence. Fewer c-bets and, when you bet, bigger.
  • Flops doblados and monocolor: their own adjustments; the equilibrium splits between checking and betting small.

After the flop

The second barrel needs value or a draw: against a small turn bet people fold much less than on the flop. In every hand the simulator shows you what the equilibrium does with your exact hand on that flop.
