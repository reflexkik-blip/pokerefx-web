The squeeze in tournament poker: when to re-raise after an open and a call

What a squeeze is, how often to do it by position and stack (a GTO table computed with a solver) and which hands to use for value and as bluffs in MTTs.

The squeeze is a 3-bet in a pot where someone has opened and another player has called. It is one of the most profitable plays in tournament poker: the opener is caught apretado between your re-raise and the caller, and the caller almost never has a strong hand (with one they would have re-raised).

What the table shows

  • From the BB against BTN + SB you re-raise about one hand in four with 14-40 big blinds: both opponents have wide ranges and the pot is already big.
  • Against an early open (UTG + HJ) the squeeze drops to 6-13%: the opener has a strong range and so does the caller.
  • With 30 big blinds or fewer, the squeeze is usually all-in: a small squeeze leaves you no room to fold to a 4-bet.
  • With 60-100 big blinds the small squeeze comes back (10 to 12.5 big blinds against an open to 2: about 5-6 times the raise) and the range becomes polarised.

Which hands to use

With 25 big blinds, from the BB against a BTN open and an SB call, the equilibrium re-raises almost every ace (offsuit too), every pair, KQ, KJ and QJ, and high suited hands such as KTs, QTs, JTs and T9s. With 100 big blinds the range tightens: weak offsuit aces drop out and the smallest pairs switch to calling or mixing.

  • Valor: high pairs, AK, AQ; against wide ranges also AJ and KQ.
  • Faroles: hands with blockers (A5s-A2s, K9s) that don't play well in a multiway pot.
  • What not to do: calling with dominated hands (KTo, QTo) in a three-way pot out of position.

Squeeze en PKO e ICM

En PKO you re-raise a little more than in ChipEV (in this spot, from 26% to 27-30% with 25 big blinds): bounties add value to knocking players out. In the burbuja, when you are covered, the squeeze tightens because losing your stack costs more than you gain: with 40 big blinds it goes from 24% to 15%. Frequently asked questions

What is a squeeze in poker?

A 3-bet after one player opens and another calls. It squeezes the opener between your re-raise and the caller, who usually has a medium hand.

When should you squeeze all-in?

In tournaments, usually with 30 big blinds or fewer: a small squeeze with a short stack commits you anyway and gives away the option to play in position.

Which hands should you squeeze with?

Value with high pairs and AK-AQ (and wider against wide ranges), and bluffs with blockers such as A5s-A2s or K9s, which play badly in multiway pots.
