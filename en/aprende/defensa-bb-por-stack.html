Big blind defence by stack: how much to call and re-raise

What percentage of hands to defend from the BB against an open from each position with 15 to 100 big blinds in tournaments, and how many to re-raise. A table computed with a solver and links to every range.

The BB is the position that defends the most hands: you already have a blind (and in tournaments the ante) in the pot, so the opponent's open gives you a very good price. Defending too little is one of the most expensive leaks in MTTs.

Three practical rules

  • The later the opponent opens, the more you defend: against the BTN or the SB, more than half of your hands.
  • With 20-30 bb the 3-bet is usually all-in: re-raise with strong hands and with small aces and pairs that don't play well postflop.
  • With deep stacks call much more and re-raise a little less: connected and suited hands gain value.

Frequently asked questions

How many hands should I defend in the BB against the button?

In a 6-max ChipEV tournament, the equilibrium defends about half its hands or more against the button open, depending on the stack.

Should I 3-bet or call from the BB?

With 30 big blinds or fewer, most re-raises are all-in with strong hands and some bluffs with blockers; with deep stacks you call more and re-raise less.
