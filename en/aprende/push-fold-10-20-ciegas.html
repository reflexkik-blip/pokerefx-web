How to play with 10 to 20 big blinds: all-in or fold

With fewer than 20 big blinds, opening to 2 big blinds and folding to a re-raise leaves you no room. That is why much of the game becomes all-in or fold: either you take the blinds and ante without a fight, or you play a pot with your equity.

What changes the decision most

  • Position: the fewer players left to act, the wider you can go all-in. From the button or the small blind, much more than from UTG.
  • Ante: with a big blind ante there are 2.5 big blinds in the pot before the deal; stealing them is worth more and ranges widen.
  • ICM: on the bubble and at the final table, losing your stack costs more than what you win; all-in and especially calling ranges tighten.

Calling an all-in

Call when your equity against their all-in range exceeds what the pot odds ask for. You can check it with the equity calculator: put your hand against a range such as 22+, A2+, K9s+.
