3-bet ranges in tournaments: how much to re-raise by position and stack

How often to 3-bet in poker tournaments by your position, the opener's and your big blinds. A table of GTO frequencies by stack and links to every full range.

The 3-bet is the first re-raise: someone opens and you raise again. In tournaments it comes in two forms: the small 3-bet (with deep stacks) and the 3-bet all-in (with 15-30 big blinds), which is one of the most profitable plays in tournament poker.

How to build the range

  • Valor: the best hands in your range (high pairs, AK, AQ) always re-raise.
  • Faroles: hands with blockers (an ace or a king) that don't play well if you call, such as A5s-A2s or K9s.
  • Against late opens re-raise more: the BTN opens many hands that can't call your 3-bet.
  • With 20-25 bb, the 3-bet all-in replaces the small 3-bet: don't let the opponent play in position.

If besides the opener someone has called, the re-raise is a squeeze: see the squeeze guide.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 3-bet in poker?

The first re-raise before the flop: one player opens (raises) and another raises again.

When should you 3-bet all-in in tournaments?

Mostly with 15 to 30 big blinds against late-position opens, with strong hands and some hands with blockers.
