Where to start

If you are just starting, read first what GTO is and push/fold with 10-20 big blinds: they are the basics that earn the most in low buy-in tournaments. Then, defending the big blind and the c-bet by flop. When you reach the bubble, the guide on ICM will tell you when to push and when to wait.

Frequently asked questions

What is GTO in poker?

An equilibrium strategy: it cannot be exploited, even if the opponent knows what you do. It is the base from which you adjust against each opponent.

What is push/fold?

With few big blinds (about 15 or fewer) the best play is usually to go all-in or fold; the charts say with which hands.

How long does it take to improve?

With 15-20 minutes a day of charts and the simulator you notice it within a few weeks, especially in short-stack play.
