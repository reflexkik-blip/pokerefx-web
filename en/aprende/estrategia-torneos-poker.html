Poker tournament strategy: how to play each stage (MTT)

A strategy guide to poker tournaments by stage: deep stacks, mid stacks, push/fold with few big blinds, the bubble and ICM, and the final table, with the solver's ranges for each moment.

A tournament is not played the same way from start to finish: what changes is your stack en big blinds and how close you are to the money. This guide sums up what to do at each stage with numbers from our solver (6-max, 1 big blind ante in the BB).

1. Deep stacks (40 big blinds or more)

Play by position. With 40 big blinds the solver opens 23% of hands from UTG, 35% from the cutoff and 47% from the button. The small blind plays almost everything (88%), often by limping. Against a button open, the big blind defends 78%: the price the pot gives it is very good. See the charts for open by position.

2. Stacks medios (20-25 big blinds)

Open ranges barely change (UTG 22%, button 41% with 25 big blinds), but the 3-bet all-in and the small blind all-in appear (14% of its hands with 25 big blinds). It is the stage where most chips are won by re-raising all-in against wide opens: check the 3-bet ranges.

3. Few big blinds (15 or fewer): push/fold

With 14 big blinds you already go all-in first in with 7% from UTG, 26% from the button and 43% from the small blind. With 10 big blinds everything played from UTG-BTN is all-in: 22% desde UTG and 43% from the button. The big blind calls the button's all-in with 34% of its hands at 10 big blinds. The guide on push/fold and the push/fold charts lo detallan.

4. Bubble e ICM

Near the money, the chips you lose are worth more than the ones you win. The effect is huge: with 20 big blinds, against the button's all-in, the big blind calls 23% of its hands early in the tournament, but only 4% on the bubble if the opponent covers it (A-Q is a fold). Read the guide on ICM and burbuja and use the calculadora ICM.

5. Bounty tournaments (PKO)

The opponent's bounty is immediate money: you call lighter when you cover them and tighten when their bounty is big. See the guide on PKO, burbuja and mesa final.

How to train it

Memorise the ranges for your usual stack in the entrenador (5 minutes a day) and play hands in the simulador, which corrects every decision from preflop to river. And remember variance: even a good player goes through streaks of 20-30 tournaments without cashing; judge your decisions, not your results.

Frequently asked questions

How many hands are played from the button in a tournament?

With 25-40 big blinds, the solver opens between 41% and 47% of hands from the button.

When should you play push/fold?

From about 15 big blinds the all-in starts to replace the open; with 10 big blinds everything played first in is all-in.

Why do you call less on the bubble?

Because of ICM: busting just before the money costs a lot, so the chips you risk are worth more than the ones you can win.
