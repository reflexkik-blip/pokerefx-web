How to practise poker for free (without playing for money)

Ways to practise poker online for free without betting: a simulator with GTO correction, preflop charts with a practice mode, calculators and exercises. To improve at tournaments from scratch.

Playing lots of hands is not enough to improve: you need to know what you did wrong in each one. These are the most effective ways to practise poker for free, without depositing or betting.

1. A simulator that corrects you

In the PokerEFX simulator you play 6-max tournament hands against opponents with real profiles (regulars, aggressive players, recreationals) and after each decision you see the grade: correct, inaccuracy or mistake, how many big blinds you lose and why. Preflop and postflop.

2. Memorise charts with questions

In the preflop charts press “Practise this chart”: it asks you random hands from the situation you choose until you master it.

3. Calculate instead of guessing

With the equity calculator you check how your hand does against a range, and with the ICM one what your chips are worth in money near the payouts.

4. Review the spots you miss

The simulator's “My leaks” mode repeats what costs you more often. Ten minutes a day before playing pays off more than an hour of videos.

A 15-minute-a-day plan

  1. 5 minutes of questions on one chart (start with the button open).
  2. 10 minutes of simulator in your usual format.
  3. Review the day's two costliest mistakes.

PokerEFX is a training tool: no real money is played here. Play responsibly; if gambling is causing you problems, ask for help at jugarbien.es.

Frequently asked questions

Can I practise poker for free without betting money?

Yes. With a simulator like PokerEFX's you play hands against simulated opponents and get the GTO correction of every decision, without depositing or betting.

What is the best way to practise poker?

Combining simulated hands with instant correction, memorising preflop charts with questions and reviewing the costliest mistakes.

Put it into practice

What you have read sticks through practice: the simulator corrects every decision and the trainer helps you memorise the ranges.
