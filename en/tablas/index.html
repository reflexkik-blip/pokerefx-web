How they were computed

Each situation is an equilibrium between the players left in the hand, with a 1 big blind ante, computed with a regret-minimisation algorithm. In formats with payouts, chips are converted to money with ICM; in PKO the bounty value is added when you cover the opponent.

The colour of each cell is the main action and its intensity is the frequency. The dot in the corner means the hand is mixed with another action. In practice, any action that does not lose EV with your hand is correct.

How to use tournament preflop charts

Each chart shows the equilibrium strategy for one situation: your position, your effective stack and what the opponents did before you. The colours show each hand's main action (raise, call, limp, all-in or fold) and the intensity how often the solver plays it. Tap a hand to see how many big blinds each alternative loses against the best one.

Pick the format first. ChipEV is for the start of the tournament, far from the money. Bubble, ITM, mid ICM and final table apply ICM, which makes you risk less. PKO accounts for bounties. In ICM formats it matters who covers whom: “covering” means you have more chips than the opponent.

Frequently asked questions

What is a preflop chart?

A 13×13 grid of hands that shows the best action with each one in a given situation: position, stack and the opponents' previous action.

Are they GTO charts?

Yes: they are computed with our 6-max tournament solver and checked against published Nash push/fold charts.

Which stack should I pick?

The effective stack: the smaller of yours and that of the opponent who can call you, measured in big blinds.
