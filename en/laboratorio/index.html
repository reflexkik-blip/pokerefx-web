Where this data comes from

The lab summarises how tournament opponents really play, measured in thousands of real hands: how often they open and call, how often they fold by bet size and how the c-bet changes with flop texture. The data is aggregated and anonymous.

It tells you when to deviate from GTO: if a type of opponent folds much more than they should, bluffing them makes money; if they never fold, value-bet thinner and bluff less.

Frequently asked questions

What is an exploitative strategy?

One that departs from equilibrium to exploit an opponent's usual mistakes, for example folding too much to a 3-bet.

Is the data about specific players?

No: they are statistics aggregated by player type, with no names.

How do I apply it?

In the simulator the opponents play with these frequencies, so you can practise against them.
