Spot analysis

By stack depth. Here you play 34.4% of hands; with 8 bb, 35.1% and with 10 bb, 32.0%. With fewer chips each hand is worth more for the blinds and ante it steals, and the opponent risks less too: that is why the range widens as the stack gets shorter.

Borderline hands. Hands that are played by a hair (what they gain over folding): K4s (under 0.1 bb), K5s (+0.1 bb), K3s (+0.1 bb), J8s (+0.1 bb), JTo (+0.1 bb). Hands just outside: Q7s, T9o, 96s, T6s, Q9o. Against opponents who fold too much the first group earns more; against those who never fold, tighten up.

The costliest folds. The weakest-looking hands that are still played here (and what you lose by folding them): 86s (0.3 bb), 98s (0.2 bb), A2o (0.4 bb), T8s (0.3 bb). They are the classic mistake: they look weak, but the solver plays them.
