Spot analysis

By stack depth. Here you play 13.4% of hands; with 16 bb, 12.8%.

Borderline hands. Hands that are played by a hair (what they gain over folding): KQo (under 0.1 bb), QJs (under 0.1 bb), A7s (+0.1 bb), KJs (+0.1 bb), A8s (+0.4 bb). Hands just outside: A5s, A4s, A3s, A2s, KTs. Against opponents who fold too much the first group earns more; against those who never fold, tighten up.

The costliest folds. The weakest-looking hands that are still played here (and what you lose by folding them): ATo (0.4 bb), A8s (0.4 bb), AJo (0.8 bb), A9s (0.5 bb). They are the classic mistake: they look weak, but the solver plays them.

Pot odds. To call you need to win about 45.3% of the time against the range UTG goes all-in with; that is why weak-looking hands call.
