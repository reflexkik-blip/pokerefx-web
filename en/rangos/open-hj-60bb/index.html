Spot analysis

By stack depth. Here you play 28.1% of hands; with 50 bb, 28.1% and with 80 bb, 27.0%. With fewer chips each hand is worth more for the blinds and ante it steals, and the opponent risks less too: that is why the range widens as the stack gets shorter.

Borderline hands. Hands that are played by a hair (what they gain over folding): K6s (under 0.1 bb), Q7s (under 0.1 bb), J7s (under 0.1 bb), KTo (under 0.1 bb), QTo (under 0.1 bb). Hands just outside: K5s, Q6s, J9o, 65s, 22. Against opponents who fold too much the first group earns more; against those who never fold, tighten up.

The costliest folds. The weakest-looking hands that are still played here (and what you lose by folding them): 98s (0.2 bb), T8s (0.2 bb), T9s (0.4 bb), A2s (0.3 bb). They are the classic mistake: they look weak, but the solver plays them.
