Replayer FAQ

Where does Winamax save your hand history?

On Windows, in %APPDATA% › winamax › documents › accounts › your nickname › history: type %APPDATA% into the File Explorer address bar and open winamax › documents › accounts › your nickname › history. There is one .txt file per tournament and day, plus one with the tournament summary.

How do I replay a Winamax hand?

Open the tournament's .txt file, copy the hand (or upload the whole file with "Upload a .txt file") and press "Load hands". You will see the hand action by action with stacks, pot, board and the equity of every player whose cards are known.

Which poker sites are supported?

Winamax, PokerStars, GGPoker and Natural8, 888poker, partypoker and ACR / Winning Poker Network: paste or upload the history exactly as the site saves it. If a hand does not load, write to us from Contact and we will add the format.

Does it work with PokerStars and GGPoker?

Yes. In PokerStars, export your history from More › Hand History; in GGPoker, from PokerCraft › Hand History › Download. Paste or upload the file just like with Winamax.

Are my hands sent to a server?

No. The replayer runs entirely in your browser: your history never leaves your device and no sign-up is needed.
