Poker hand replayer

Paste or upload your history from Winamax o PokerStars and replay each hand action by action: stacks, bets, pot, board and the equity of every visible hand at each moment. Everything is processed in your browser; your hands never leave your device.

On Winamax, histories are in Documents › Winamax › accounts › your nickname › history. On PokerStars: More › Hand History.

Also with the ← → arrow keys. Want to know if you played the hand well? Practise the spot in the simulador.
