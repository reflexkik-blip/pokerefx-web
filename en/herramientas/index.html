What you can train on PokerEFX

PokerEFX brings together on one site everything you need to study tournament poker: 1,000+ GTO preflop charts (open, defence, 3-bet, push/fold, limps) in ChipEV, ICM and PKO; a simulador that corrects every decision from preflop to river; a range trainer with spaced repetition; a replayer hand calculadoras for equity, ICM, pot odds and variance.

The fastest way to improve: memorise the ranges for your usual stack in the trainer (5 minutes a day), play hands in the simulator and review the spots you miss in “My leaks” mode.
