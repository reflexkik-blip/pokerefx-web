Privacy policy

PokerEFX works without sign-up: your progress (simulator decisions, settings, theme) is saved in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device.

If you create an account, we will store your email and your progress to sync it across devices. You can delete your account and your data at any time from “My account”.

Advertising: the site is funded by Google AdSense. If you accept advertising cookies, Google may show you personalised ads; if not, it only shows non-personalised ads, which use cookies only to limit frequency and prevent fraud. You can change your choice at any time from “Cookie preferences”.

We do not sell personal data. To exercise your rights (access, rectification, erasure, objection) write to the contact listed in the legal notice.
